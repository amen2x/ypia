# Tigerhacks2026 - Y.P.I.A (Your Parent Is Aging)
<!-- your parent is aging :/ -->
As our loved ones age, it is hard to tell when they expereince congnitive or physicial decline. These changes are very minimual, most of the time the aging parent would blame it on a lack of sleep. But, in reality it could be due to a slow realization that they cannot process, comprehend, and  recall information as fast as they used to. 

## Our Goal. 
That's why we introduce Y.P.I.A (Your Parent Is Aging), with Y.P.I.A you are able to check up on your parent.
